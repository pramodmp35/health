Answers to the most common admin questions.

Open the Claims tab from the sidebar, click a claim, and use the Approve or Decline buttons at the top-right of the detail panel. Every action is logged in the activity feed automatically.
Go to Members and click "Add Member" at the top-right. Fill in their profile, choose a plan, and their welcome email is sent automatically.
Yes. Open the Reports tab and click the download icon next to any report. CSV, PDF, and XLSX exports are all supported for most report types.
Sign out and log back in as a different role. The role toggle on the login page determines which dashboard you land on — dashboard-admin.html or dashboard-user.html.
Member messages, ratings, and survey responses are surfaced in the Messages tab and aggregated on the Analytics page. Filter by channel to see email vs. in-app conversations.
